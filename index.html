About us

Almost twenty years in global financial markets.

Pilgrim Fund was founded in 2004 as a privately owned investment manager and adviser, and has operated in global financial markets for almost 20 years.

The firm has been featured in online and print publications and is tracked on the Bloomberg platform.

Our objective is to balance alpha generation with capital preservation, in pursuit of long-term capital growth.

Our approach

Two objectives, held in balance.

01

Alpha generation

Seeking returns beyond the market through active, research-led investment decisions.

02

Capital preservation

Protecting the downside, so that long-term growth compounds rather than resets.
